# BRAVE HITTER ACADEMY — WEEKLY MISSIONS & TRACKING

This file is authoritative for the 13-page Weekly Missions section.

The Weekly Missions are a standalone section after the Confidence Toolkit.
They are not chapter pages. Do not add page numbers.

Every weekly mission page contains:
- Week number and title
- This Week's Mission
- Three challenges
- Monday–Sunday tracker
- Days completed
- One reflection question
- Coach Mark Says

## PAGE 1 — DIVIDER / HOW IT WORKS
Each week, practice one mental skill from the Academy.

Complete the three challenges, mark the days you practiced, and answer the reflection question.

Do not chase a perfect week. The goal is repetition: one small Brave Hitter choice at a time.
