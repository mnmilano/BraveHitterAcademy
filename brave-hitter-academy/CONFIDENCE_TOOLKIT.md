## PAGE 1 — YOUR CONFIDENCE TOOLKIT

Use these tools whenever baseball feels difficult.

### HOW TO USE IT
You do not need every tool at once. Pick the one that matches the moment.

Before a game, use a preparation tool. After a mistake, use a reset tool. When confidence drops, use a courage tool.

The goal is not to make every nervous feeling disappear. The goal is to know what to do next.

### TOOL 1 — VISUALIZATION
Picture a confident at-bat before it happens.
See the pitch. See your load. See a Brave Swing.

### TOOL 2 — NEXT PITCH
Breathe. Learn one thing. Let it go.
Return your attention to the next opportunity.

### TOOL 3 — BRAVE SWINGS
Reward committed swings at good pitches.
Courage first. Results second.

## PAGE 2 — SEE IT EARLY. HIT IT HARD.

### TOOL 4 — FAST PITCH HERO
**WHAT IT IS** — A simple attention tool for facing faster pitching.

**WHY IT WORKS** — Fear makes you focus on speed. Tracking the release point gives your brain useful information.

**HOW TO DO IT** — Get ready early. Find the pitcher's release point. Track the ball as long as you can.

**GIO'S EXAMPLE** — Instead of thinking “He's too fast,” Gio tells himself: “Early eyes.”

### TOOL 5 — HARD CONTACT CHALLENGE
**WHAT IT IS** — Judge the quality of contact instead of whether the ball becomes a hit.

**WHY IT WORKS** — Fielders and luck affect hits. Your pitch choice and swing quality are more controllable.

**HOW TO DO IT** — Count line drives and well-struck balls in practice or games.

**GIO'S EXAMPLE** — A line drive caught by the shortstop still earns credit for hard contact.

### COACH MARK'S QUICK CHECK
Fast pitch? → EARLY EYES.

Worried about your average? → CHASE HARD CONTACT.

## PAGE 3 — THE CONFIDENCE JOURNAL

Confidence grows faster when you notice evidence of your progress.

### WHAT IT IS
A short record of things you did well, learned, or handled bravely.

This is not a batting-average tracker. It is evidence that you are growing.

### WHEN TO USE IT
- After practice
- After a game
- After a difficult baseball day
- Any time your brain starts saying, “I'm not getting better.”

### THE 3-WIN RULE
Write three wins.

A win can be effort, courage, a good decision, a reset, hard contact, or helping a teammate.

### MY THREE WINS TODAY
1. ________________________________________________
2. ________________________________________________
3. ________________________________________________

Something I learned: ________________________________________________

## PAGE 4 — YOUR SUPERPOWER ROUTINE

A repeatable routine gives your brain something familiar to do.

### BUILD YOUR ROUTINE
1. **BREATHE** — One slow breath.
2. **FOCUS** — Pick one simple cue.
3. **SEE** — Look at the pitcher and find the ball.
4. **TRUST** — Step in ready to compete.

Keep it short enough to repeat before every at-bat.

### CHOOSE YOUR FOCUS PHRASE
- [ ] See it. Trust it.
- [ ] Early eyes.
- [ ] Take my swing.
- [ ] Be brave.
- [ ] I've done the work.
- [ ] My own: ______________________________

### GIO'S ROUTINE
Breathe → look at the barrel → “See it. Trust it.” → eyes to the pitcher → compete.

### COACH MARK SAYS
A routine doesn't guarantee a hit. It gets you ready for your opportunity.

## PAGE 5 — POSITIVE TEAMMATE LEADERSHIP

Confidence gets stronger when you help someone else compete.

### THREE WAYS TO LEAD
1. **ENCOURAGE** — Help a teammate after a mistake.
2. **CELEBRATE** — Be genuinely excited when someone else succeeds.
3. **HELP** — Find something useful to do without being asked.

You do not need to be the captain or best player to lead.

### TRY THESE PHRASES
“Next pitch—you've got this.”
“Great swing.”
“Keep competing.”
“We'll get the next one.”
“Nice job staying with it.”

Use words that help teammates return to ready.

### COACH MARK SAYS
Great teammates change the energy around them.

## PAGE 6 — MY BRAVE HITTER RESET CARD

When you don't know what to do, start here.

### I'M NERVOUS
1. One slow breath.
2. Pick one focus phrase.
3. Find the ball.
4. Take your best swing.

### I MADE A MISTAKE
1. Feel it.
2. Learn one thing.
3. Say “Next pitch.”
4. Return to ready.

### THE PITCHER IS FAST
1. Get ready earlier.
2. Find the release point.
3. Track the ball.
4. Trust your swing.

### I'M NOT GETTING HITS
- Stop chasing hits.
- Hunt good pitches.
- Track hard contact.
- Write three wins.

### I'M LOSING CONFIDENCE
- Read your Confidence Journal.
- Remember your preparation.
- Do one brave thing.
- Confidence follows courage.

### MY TEAMMATE IS DOWN
- Notice them.
- Encourage them.
- Celebrate their next brave action.
- Help them reset.
