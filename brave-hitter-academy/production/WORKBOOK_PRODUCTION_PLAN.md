# Brave Hitter Academy — Workbook Production Plan

**Target:** exactly 162 pages at 11 × 8.5 inches landscape / 792 × 612 PDF points  
**Production method:** deterministic editable layouts with real typeset copy; AI imagery limited to controlled supporting artwork  
**Gate at plan creation:** pre-production READY; Chapter 1 masters READY

**Current gate — 2026-09-08:** Chapter 1 is user-approved and frozen. Chapters 2–12 and Front Matter passed their isolated production gates. The six-page Confidence Toolkit passes 55/55 automated checks plus internal content, visual, character, and writing-space review and is ready for user review.

## Global Controls

- Authoritative copy comes only from the applicable approved Markdown source.
- Chapter pages inherit the corresponding Chapter 1 page family and geometry.
- Canonical Gio and Coach Mark are reused; they are not regenerated unless a future required pose cannot be derived without drift and the user approves that specific replacement.
- Gio is locked as a right-handed batter and thrower. Every action image must pass real-baseball review for handedness, grip, stance, glove hand, bat/throw path, ball direction, plate/field geometry, and camera viewpoint.
- Every generated art component is staged, visually inspected, and promoted only after passing character, anatomy, branding, text, and transparency checks.
- Every page is rendered at 3300 × 2550 for visual QC and exported at 792 × 612 points in the assembled PDF.
- Staging/originals and contact sheets are excluded from publication output.
