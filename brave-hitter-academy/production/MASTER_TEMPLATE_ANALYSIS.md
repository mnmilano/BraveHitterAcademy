## Shared Page System

- **Geometry:** 3300 × 2550 pixels; exact 11 × 8.5-inch landscape ratio; 300-PPI raster target.
- **Safe area:** primary copy begins approximately 100–150 pixels inside trim; critical text avoids the outer 100 pixels.
- **Palette:** cream paper field, deep black structure, baseball red hierarchy, white body contrast, limited gold callouts.
- **Typography:** DIN Condensed/Impact-style display hierarchy; Avenir Next/Helvetica-style body copy.
- **Texture:** subtle deterministic paper grain; selective paint strokes, rough curves, simple athletic shapes.
- **Header:** Pages 2–7 use a 180-pixel black band, red Chapter 1 tab, white page-family label, and restrained red slash.
- **Footer:** Pages 2–7 use a 290–410-pixel rough black field with red terminal shape, star marker, primary takeaway, and gold chapter principle. No printed page number.
- **Character rules:** Gio is red uniform/cap or helmet with #13; Coach Mark is red cap, orange/yellow sunglasses, salt-and-pepper beard, black Academy top.
- **Fixed elements:** page geometry, palette, display/body relationship, family header, footer rhythm, safe margins, and page-family composition.
- **Variable elements:** chapter number/title, narrative/lesson copy, character pose, supporting environment, prompt wording, takeaway statements.

## Page 1 — Chapter Cover

- **Purpose:** introduce chapter theme with premium hero energy and minimal instructional copy.
- **Grid:** approximately 52% cream editorial field / 48% cinematic environment and hero art, separated by a large concave boundary.
- **Title zone:** upper-left crest and chapter number; large stacked title below a red paint stroke.
- **Text zone:** three-line preview below title; compact black core-rule panel in the lower-left.
- **Character:** Gio is the dominant right-side hero, about 45–55% page height, grounded in the environment.
- **Environment:** full-height right-side stadium field, cropped rather than stretched.
- **Decorations:** one major red brush stroke, red underline, minimal bottom identity band.
- **Density:** low-to-medium; cinematic space is intentional.
- **Fixed:** crest position, stacked-title hierarchy, split composition, core-rule panel, bottom band.
- **Variable:** chapter title, preview, core rule, environment, Gio pose.
- **Anti-drift:** no activity, tracker, long lesson copy, page number, team branding, or crowded panel stack.

## Page 2 — Story, Part 1

- **Purpose:** open the chapter narrative and establish the mental-performance problem.
- **Grid:** roughly 51% cream narrative field / 49% major illustration, joined by an irregular curved/painted edge.
- **Titles:** fixed family header; large story title over a red brush mark.
- **Text zone:** single readable column, approximately 14–18 deliberately broken lines.
- **Illustration:** major Gio scene on the right, with no generated body text or signage.
- **Callout:** compact lower-left black reminder panel.
- **Density:** medium; narrative remains primary and readable.
- **Fixed:** header, narrative column width, large illustration balance, lower callout, footer.
- **Variable:** story title/copy, Gio pose, environment, concise callout.
- **Anti-drift:** no prompts, trackers, concept cards, parent guidance, or second activity column.

## Page 3 — Story, Part 2

- **Purpose:** continue and resolve the story while preserving the approved outcome.
- **Grid:** inherits Page 2, with similar copy/illustration balance and continuous visual rhythm.
- **Titles:** fixed family header and large story-resolution title.
- **Text zone:** one readable narrative column with the outcome and Coach Mark moment.
- **Illustration:** Gio action scene on the right; action supports but does not rewrite the outcome.
- **Callout:** red Coach Mark teaching banner near the lower copy field.
- **Density:** medium; concise resolution and visual action.
- **Fixed:** Page 2 counterpart geometry, header/footer, single narrative column, lower teaching banner.
- **Variable:** resolution copy, action pose, environment, Coach Mark cue.
- **Anti-drift:** never turn an approved out into a hit/run/home run; no activity or takeaway list.

## Page 4 — What Gio Learned

- **Purpose:** translate the story into clear mental-performance concepts.
- **Grid:** full-width cream editorial field; three equal concept cards across the upper middle; two wider lesson regions below; Coach Mark anchors the right.
- **Titles:** family header, large lesson headline, red underline.
- **Concept zone:** three concise numbered cards with red headers.
- **Why It Matters:** large black panel with gold header and short white copy.
- **Real Baseball Example:** outlined cream panel; text only.
- **Character:** Coach Mark appears as a teaching anchor without covering lesson copy.
- **Density:** medium-high but strongly segmented and scan-friendly.
- **Fixed:** three concept slots, Why It Matters, Real Baseball Example, Coach Mark zone, footer.
- **Variable:** concept titles/copy, lesson headline, example, Coach Mark cue.
- **Anti-drift:** Real Baseball Example remains text-only; no tracker, worksheet, professional player, or generic “The Lesson” substitution.

## Page 5 — Parent Dugout

- **Purpose:** give parents chapter-specific language and reinforcement guidance.
- **Grid:** strong left praise panel, center questions panel, right character/avoid zone, lower reminder strip.
- **Titles:** family header, red parent-focused headline, concise purpose sentence.
- **Guidance zones:** praise/reinforce items; practical questions; avoid guidance; parent reminder.
- **Character:** Coach Mark is secondary to guidance, placed to the right.
- **Density:** medium; editorial rather than worksheet-like.
- **Fixed:** four guidance functions, clear parent voice, character support, footer.
- **Variable:** chapter-specific praise items, questions, avoid guidance, reminder.
- **Anti-drift:** no child reflection prompts, Weekly Mission, journal scorecard, generic parenting filler, or statistics emphasis.

## Page 6 — Reflection Time

- **Purpose:** provide meaningful player reflection with usable handwriting space.
- **Grid:** wide left/center writing field and restrained right-side support art/callout.
- **Titles:** family header, red three-beat headline, one-sentence invitation.
- **Prompt zones:** five numbered prompts with broad horizontal writing lines.
- **Character:** smaller Gio support image on right; never dominates writing space.
- **Density:** deliberately low; whitespace is functional.
- **Fixed:** five-prompt capacity, line spacing, reduced decoration, footer.
- **Variable:** authoritative chapter prompts and small support art.
- **Anti-drift:** do not add mission tracking, cramped lines, decorative panels over writing space, or replace with a weekly activity.

## Page 7 — Key Takeaways

- **Purpose:** close the chapter with memorable principles and encouragement.
- **Grid:** approximately 58% takeaway list / 42% Gio hero close; full-width black mantra band above footer.
- **Titles:** family header and red closing headline.
- **Takeaway zone:** five numbered principles with strong typographic hierarchy.
- **Character:** Gio in a confident hero position with a restrained low-opacity burst.
- **Mantra:** black lower panel repeats the chapter's core rule.
- **Density:** low-to-medium; decisive episode-ending rhythm.
- **Fixed:** short takeaway list, confident Gio, mantra band, footer.
- **Variable:** authoritative takeaway statements, hero pose, mantra.
- **Anti-drift:** no journal fields, mission tracker, long teaching paragraphs, or new curriculum.

## Cross-Page Anti-Drift Checks

1. Page family and purpose remain unchanged.
2. Copy comes from the authoritative content file for that chapter.
3. Page 2 and Page 3 remain narrative; Page 4 remains instruction; Page 5 remains parent guidance; Page 6 remains writing; Page 7 remains recap.
4. All text is deterministic and spell-checked; image generation supplies artwork only.
5. No page number, binding simulation, professional branding, pseudo-text, or unapproved logo.
6. Gio is always #13 and visually canonical; Coach Mark retains all locked markers.
7. Gio always bats and throws right-handed; batting/fielding illustrations must pass mechanics, plate/field geometry, and ball-direction review and must never be mirrored into the wrong handedness.
8. The cream/red/black system, title scale, header/footer rhythm, safe margins, and writing-space behavior remain consistent.
9. New chapter art may vary, but visual novelty may not alter curriculum or page architecture.
