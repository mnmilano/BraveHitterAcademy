# BRAVE HITTER ACADEMY — PRODUCTION RULES

## Mission

Produce the complete, publication-quality **Brave Hitter Academy** workbook from the approved sources in `brave-hitter-academy/`.

This is production, not concept development. The curriculum, storyline, characters, visual identity, page architecture, section structure, and approved assets are established. Execute them faithfully.

Do not rewrite, reinterpret, expand, simplify, reorganize, or “improve” approved material without explicit user approval. When creative freedom conflicts with consistency, **consistency wins**.

## Final workbook

The finished workbook contains exactly **162 pages**:

| Section | Pages |
|---|---:|
| Front Matter | 13 |
| Chapters 1–12: 12 × 7 | 84 |
| Confidence Toolkit | 6 |

| Game Day Journal: 20 × 2 | 40 |

No section may be omitted, merged, or silently shortened. The workbook is incomplete until all 162 pages pass content, visual, and technical QC.

## Source priority

1. User’s latest explicit instruction.
2. `WORKBOOK_MASTER_STRUCTURE.md` for pagination, section order, and completeness.
3. Applicable content file: `APPROVED_STORYLINE.md`, `FRONT_MATTER.md`, `CONFIDENCE_TOOLKIT.md`, `WEEKLY_MISSIONS.md`, `GAME_DAY_JOURNAL.md`, or `CLOSING_GRADUATION.md`.
4. Corresponding approved Chapter 1 page for chapter composition, hierarchy, density, spacing, and visual behavior.
5. `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` for format and visual rules.
6. `CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` for character identity and continuity.
7. `ASSET_MANIFEST.md` for asset paths, classifications, priority, and missing-asset behavior.
8. Verified approved assets, then verified secondary references.

Written sources control **what** a page says. Approved masters and the design system control **how** it is presented. A lower source cannot override a higher one.

For unresolved conflicts, document the exact conflict and affected pages, then ask the user. Never choose silently.

## Content rules

You may tighten copy to fit an approved template, fix obvious language errors, and adjust deterministic typesetting without changing meaning.

You may not:

- change a topic, lesson, story outcome, mission, or mental-performance principle;
- invent curriculum, exercises, trackers, worksheets, panels, or filler;
- replace mental-performance lessons with hitting-mechanics lessons;
- let an image model determine curriculum or final copy;
- merge Weekly Missions, Confidence Toolkit, or Game Day Journal content into chapter pages;
- move parent guidance into child reflections or vice versa;
- add real-player claims, quotations, statistics, endorsements, or protected team branding without approval;
- use rejected or obsolete material as current content.

If copy does not fit, tighten it without changing meaning. Do not add panels, shrink text below readable size, remove required writing space, or redesign the page.

## Locked chapter system

Every chapter contains exactly:

1. Chapter Cover
2. Story — Part 1
3. Story — Part 2
4. What Gio Learned
5. Parent Dugout
6. Reflection Time
7. Key Takeaways

For Chapters 2–12, Page N inherits approved Chapter 1 Page N. Change chapter-specific content and approved supporting artwork, not the underlying design.

- **Cover:** chapter number/title, Gio hero art, suitable environment, concise preview.
- **Story 1:** narrative plus major illustration; no activities or trackers.
- **Story 2:** narrative continuation and concise Coach Mark moment when supported.
- **What Gio Learned:** clear concepts, **Why It Matters**, and text-only **Real Baseball Example**.
- **Parent Dugout:** parent guidance on what to reinforce, say, ask, praise, or avoid.
- **Reflection Time:** meaningful prompts with generous usable writing space.
- **Key Takeaways:** concise recap, memorable principles/mantras, and encouragement.

Page 5 is not a combined reflection page. Page 6 is not a Weekly Mission. Page 7 is not a Game Day Journal page.

## Format and visual system

Every finished page must be:

- 11 × 8.5 inches, landscape;
- approximately 1.294:1;
- 792 × 612 points in the PDF;
- independently composed at native landscape size;
- consistent in apparent scale, margins, safe areas, and trim behavior.

Never stretch portrait/square art, crop contact sheets into pages, use collages as page sources, simulate coil binding, or print page numbers—including obsolete `Page X of 72` elements.

Follow `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`: red/black/cream/white palette, cream paper texture, distressed athletic typography, red/black brushwork, polished illustrated comic/sports aesthetic, selective cinematic environments, halftone/dirt/ink/burst/speed-line motifs, strong hierarchy, proper writing space, approved crest, and consistent footer treatment.

Avoid generic worksheet layouts dominated by repetitive white rounded rectangles. Pages must look fully art-directed, not like visible skeleton templates.

## Character continuity

Gio must match the canonical reference: established face, age, proportions, style, red uniform, and jersey **#13**. Use an activity-appropriate cap or helmet. Never add jersey pseudo-text or an invented name.

Gio is always a **right-handed batter and right-handed thrower**. Batting art must place him in the right-handed batter's box with coherent grip, stance, rotation, bat path, plate/field geometry, and ball direction. Fielding art must place the glove on his left hand and show throws from his right hand. Reject mirrored, mechanically impossible, or directionally inconsistent baseball action.

Coach Mark must match the canonical reference: red cap, yellow/orange sunglasses on the cap, short salt-and-pepper beard, black Academy shirt/hoodie, and friendly, calm, confident presence.

Reject character drift, malformed anatomy, duplicate limbs, warped equipment, inconsistent clothing, unreadable faces, pseudo-text, or style mismatch. Follow `CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` and `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.

## Asset rules

Use assets classified **REQUIRED** or **APPROVED / REUSABLE** in `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`. Optional references are for recovery or conditioning only. Keep obsolete assets outside production paths.

If a required asset is missing:

1. stop the affected page or scene;
2. search canonical names, original names, and legacy handles;
3. record the missing item and affected pages;
4. continue only unrelated work;
5. do not silently invent or substitute;
6. obtain approval before generating a replacement;
7. stage candidates outside canonical paths;
8. promote only after approval and QC.

Approved Chapter 1 Pages 5–7 are mandatory. If missing or unverified, Chapters 2–12 are not fully template-ready.

## Image generation

Use image generation for missing art components, not final copy, autonomous curriculum, or multi-page production.

For character art, condition on the canonical cutout and closest approved pose. Add the applicable page master when placement/rendering treatment matters. Generate one controlled asset/scene at a time with no labels, body text, footer, crest, page number, or layout panels. Inspect identity, face, hands, anatomy, clothing, #13, cap/helmet, bat, and glove.

Environment art must contain no people, text, signage, logos, or page furniture and must support native landscape placement. Icons/decorations must be isolated, reusable, text-free, clear at small sizes, and consistent with the palette.

Never ask one generation to create several finished pages. Never use AI-generated page text in final production.

## Deterministic construction

Assemble final pages with deterministic layout and real typeset text. Preserve exact geometry, editable text, consistent styles/grids/safe areas, undistorted assets, repeatable template population, and print-ready export.

AI imagery may supply illustrations, backgrounds, textures, or isolated decorations. It may not replace typesetting, pagination, page assembly, or preflight.

## Page QC

Each page must pass:

- correct source, purpose, and approved meaning;
- no invented content or drift;
- accurate language and line breaks;
- correct template, density, hierarchy, whitespace, and writing space;
- consistent crest, palette, typography, texture, brushwork, footer, and styling;
- no unintended crop, coil graphic, or printed page number;
- canonical characters and approved assets only;
- correct Gio #13, anatomy, clothing, cap/helmet, equipment, faces, and hands;
- no stretched/contact-sheet assets, pseudo-text, watermark, or unwanted logo;
- exact 11 × 8.5 landscape / 792 × 612 point geometry;
- adequate resolution, clean transparency, sharp text, safe margins, successful rendering, and no missing fonts/assets.

After every chapter/section, render pages consistently, inspect side by side for drift, verify order/count, compare chapter pages with Chapter 1 counterparts, and record failures/corrections in `brave-hitter-academy/qc/`.

For workbook production and QC, follow `QC_POLICY.md`.
Read it before generating, inspecting, repairing, or regenerating workbook pages.
